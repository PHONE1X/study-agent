# Session glossary: one term per line, "#" for comments.
#
# Copy to ~/.config/study-agent/glossary.txt and fill in the names, products and
# jargon of the session you are about to record.
#
# Why this exists. Whisper biases word choice towards whatever appears in its
# prompt. Without a glossary, a product name it has never seen comes out as a
# phonetically similar word — and the note-writing model does not flag the word
# it failed to recognise. It invents a confident definition for it instead, so a
# speech-recognition error becomes a fabricated fact in your study notes.
#
# The file is re-read while running. When you hear a term come out mangled, add
# it and the next window picks it up — no restart.
#
# Keep it short, roughly 300 characters total. It shares whisper's prompt window
# with the tail of the previous phrase, and crowding that out costs you
# punctuation.
